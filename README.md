# Metrify

Drop a session. Metrify sorts it into the house layout (drums, bass, guitars, keys, synths, vocals, each in its own coloured group) and makes it in **Ableton Live**, **Pro Tools** or **Logic**. You can also preview a session, a pile of stems or an Ableton Move set without opening the DAW.

## ⬇ [Download Metrify](https://github.com/whoisthemetro/metrify-download/releases/latest/download/Metrify.zip)

That link always gets the newest version.

### Install
1. Open **Metrify.zip** to unzip it, then drag **Metrify** into your **Applications** folder.
2. The first time you open it, macOS may say it can't check it for malicious software, because Metrify isn't from the App Store. Right-click Metrify, choose **Open**, then click **Open** again. Or go to **System Settings › Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**.
3. To make Pro Tools or Logic sessions, Metrify needs **System Settings › Privacy & Security › Accessibility**. It asks for this the first time.

Metrify runs on Macs with Apple silicon (M1 or newer).
